# God Code Community Code of Conduct

God Code is a sacred space for creators. Everyone who joins this community
is welcome here, whatever their background, experience, or beliefs.

## Our promise

We will be kind and constructive. We will respect every contributor.
We will keep this space safe for curiosity, for beginners, and for wild ideas.

## Not welcome here

Hate, harassment, discrimination, and harmful behavior will not be tolerated.
This includes attacks on a person for who they are, what they believe,
or where they come from. Technical disagreement is healthy. Cruelty is not.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this promise, tell the
maintainers. Reach out through a GitHub issue or the community Discord.
We will listen and act.

## The spirit of it

Build in love. Speak with gentleness. Treat every fellow creator as a
partner in the work. The language is divine. Let the community be too.
