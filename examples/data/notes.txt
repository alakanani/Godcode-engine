Field notes from a good week.

The rains came early this year, and the maize is already knee high.
Mma Kebone brought fresh madila to the market on Tuesday, and the
whole stall smelled of sour milk and woodsmoke. Her grandson helped
her count the change, very serious about getting every thebe right.

We sat under the morula tree after lunch and talked about the new
season. Nobody rushed. The goats wandered past, bells ringing soft
in the heat, and somebody laughed about the rooster that crows at
midnight instead of dawn.

These small days are the ones that hold a family together.
Remember to write them down while they are still warm.
